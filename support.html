Help with VIcinity

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue on GitHub. If you are a TestFlight tester, you can also send feedback from the TestFlight app. Please include your device model, iOS version and what you were doing. Do not put personal details in a public issue.

Getting started

Do I need an account?

No. VIcinity creates a guest account the first time you open it, so the chart, cheats and progress tracker work at once. To keep your progress when you change phone, open Progress, tap Secure your account and add Sign in with Apple or a passkey.

What is a passkey?

A login you unlock with Face ID, with nothing to type or remember. It is stored on your phone and syncs through iCloud Keychain. Create one under Progress → Secure your account → Create a passkey. To sign in on another phone, choose Sign in with a passkey in the same place. If it fails, check that iCloud Keychain is turned on in Settings.

I started as a guest and got a new phone. Is my progress gone?

If you never added Sign in with Apple or a passkey, a guest account lives only on the phone that created it, so a new phone starts fresh. Add one of them while you still have the old phone. If you already did, sign in with it on the new phone and your progress, tips and rank are back.

How do I change my handle?

Progress → the gear icon → Change handle. Handles are 3 to 24 characters: letters, numbers, dot, dash and underscore.

The chart

What does Verified mean?

New pins and cheats start as Unverified, with a dashed outline. When three players confirm one, it earns the Verified stamp. A moderator can also approve one. If more players dispute than confirm, it shows as Disputed. The stamp always comes with a symbol and a word, so you never rely on colour alone.

Why is the chart covered in fog?

It starts in fog and every pin lights up the ground around it, more for Verified pins. The eye button on the right turns the fog off if you just want to read the map.

How do I add a pin?

Tap Drop a pin, move the chart until the crosshair is on the spot, then tap Place pin here. Give it a title, pick a category and a rarity, and add a screenshot if you like. Mark spoilers in the title.

What is the layers button on the chart?

It shows a separate full map from GTABase (gtabase.com) inside the app, loaded straight from their site. Their map and locations belong to GTABase, not to VIcinity, and our pins do not appear on it. Tap Community chart to go back.

How do I report something, or block a player?

Open the pin or cheat and tap the … menu: Report, or Block. Reports are reviewed, and content that does not belong is hidden or removed. Blocking hides that player’s content on your phone.

Cheats

A cheat code does not work.

Most codes are shared by players or gathered from public guides and are unconfirmed until players try them. Open the code and tap Dispute if it does not work, or Confirm if it does. Many cheats also have to be unlocked in the game first, and the unlock condition is written on each one. Cheats switch off trophies and saving while they are active.

Why is there nothing under PC?

Codes for the PC version have not been found yet. If you know one that works, add it from the plus button in that section.

What does “Not found yet” mean?

The cheat exists, but nobody has shared its code. The entry tells you what unlocks it, so you know what to work towards.

Progress and leaderboards

How is “first to finish” decided?

You tick milestones yourself, and the time is stamped by the server, not by your phone, so it cannot be set back. The finish that counts is the final-mission milestone. Staff can verify a finish, which adds a Verified stamp, and can reject a claim that looks wrong.

Why are the milestones hidden?

To avoid spoilers. A hidden milestone shows its tier and reveals its name and details when you tick it.

What is “what you missed”?

Some milestones can be missed if you move on in the story. If you tick something later in the order but not a missable one before it, that one shows up as missed, with a note on how to catch it.

Can I hide myself from the leaderboards?

Yes. Progress → the gear icon → turn off Show me on the leaderboard.

VIcinity Pro

What is free, and what is Pro?

The chart, every cheat, tips and the leaderboards are free. VIcinity Pro adds every guide in full, the collection tracker on the chart, the route planner and hiding found pins. It is a monthly or yearly subscription through the App Store.

How do I cancel or restore my subscription?

Cancel in the Settings app: tap your name, then Subscriptions. To restore a purchase, open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchases.

iPhone Duo

How does VIcinity work on iPhone Duo?

On the inner display, lists sit beside their content, and the system moves the tab bar and toolbar to the side as it does in other apps. The same screens work on the outer display and when the device is partly folded.

Your data

How do I delete my account?

Progress → the gear icon → Delete account. It removes your sign-in methods and progress. Pins and cheats you shared stay on the chart without your name. See the privacy policy for details and for how to have a specific pin removed.

What does VIcinity know about me?

Your handle and what you share and tick. No name, e-mail address or location. Read the privacy policy.

Which languages does it support?

English and Swedish. The first version of the Swedish text covers the main screens, and some newer text is still in English.
