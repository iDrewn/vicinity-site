The chart

Fog of war, by the community.

Every pin lights up the ground around it. A find that three players confirm earns the Verified stamp, so you can see what to trust at a glance.

Progress

How far have you got?

Tick milestones as you play, log what you collect, and see who finished first. Finish times come from the server’s clock, so the board stays fair.

iPhone Duo

Two screens. One chart.

Open the Duo and the list sits beside the content, with the tab bar on the side where iOS puts it. Close it and the same screens collapse to one, with nothing hidden.

Details

Built to be trusted.

Anyone can add a pin or a cheat. Everyone can check it.

Verified

Unverified until it’s proven.

New pins and cheats start as Unverified. Players confirm or dispute them, and three confirmations earn the Verified stamp. Reports are reviewed, and content that doesn’t belong is removed.

Cheats

By platform.

PS5, Xbox and PC each get their own list, with the unlock condition for every code.

Community

Tip each other off.

Share rare finds and say thanks to the players who helped you. Thanks build a tipster rank.

Sign in

No password to remember.

Start as a guest at once. Add Sign in with Apple or a passkey when you want your progress on every device.

Pro

Free to use. Pro to go deeper.

The chart, every cheat and the leaderboards are free. VIcinity Pro adds every guide in full, the collection tracker and a route planner, as a monthly or yearly subscription.

Private

No ads. No tracking.

No ads, no analytics and no name or e-mail address needed. Read the privacy policy.
